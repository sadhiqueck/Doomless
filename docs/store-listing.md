## Title
Focus Extension — Block the Scroll, Swap in Your Work

## Short Description (132 characters)
Hides distracting social feeds on YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok. Instantly swaps in a 30-second swipeable sticky note of your work.

## Category
Productivity

## Detailed Description
Most website blockers simply replace social media with a blank wall — leaving you staring into space or reflexively opening another tab.

Focus Extension bridges the real gap: **intercept the reach-for-the-scroll moment and instantly swap in a 30-second swipeable unit of your own material.**

Not pre-made student flashcards, but your actual work: today's sprint ticket, today's chapter, today's deadline.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Instant Feed Hiding:** Pure web-tech DOM hiding injected before initial render (no feed flash) for YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.
- **Sticky Note Swap Layer:** Floating pastel sticky note with your real task checklist, progress bar, and 5 pastel color themes.
- **Card Types:** Task cards with checkboxes, 30-second read summaries, and 5-minute Pomodoro sprints.
- **Flexible Ingestion:** Paste today's standup notes, drag-and-drop a Markdown file, or import directly from public GitHub Issues.
- **Shame-Free Cooldown:** Need a quick break? "Let me through" provides an exact 5-minute unlock before gently re-arming.
- **100% Private:** No cloud accounts, no tracking, zero analytics. Everything stays strictly inside `chrome.storage.local`.

### How to Use:
1. Click the Focus icon in your toolbar to see today's remaining tasks.
2. Open Settings to paste your daily checklist or sync your notes.
3. Open YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok — and your work is right there waiting for you.
