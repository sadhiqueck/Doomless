Privacy Policy for Focus Extension

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Zero Data Collection

Focus Extension operates 100% locally in your browser. We do not track, collect, transmit, or monetize any of your browsing history, tasks, notes, or keystrokes.

Local Storage Only

All data you input (tasks, sprint tickets, schedules, preferences) is stored strictly on your device using chrome.storage.local.

Permissions

Host permissions for YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok are used exclusively to inject client-side CSS rules that hide feed DOM nodes and present your task note.

Contact

For questions or support, reach out to dev@focusextension.app.
